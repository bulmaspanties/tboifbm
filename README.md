**Gridiron** suits Isaac up for football. Every item is real gear with its own mechanic: no reskins, no stat-only filler, nothing replaced.
Tackle with the helmet, deflect with the pads, catch with the gloves, punt with the tee, and when you're wearing three pieces you're **Suited Up**.
Then take on a rival Linebacker, a giant Mascot, a stadium full of grotesque football enemies, and **The Franchise**.

**[⬇ Download the latest release](https://github.com/bulmaspanties/tboifbm/releases/latest)**, unzip it into your `mods` folder, and enable **Gridiron**.

## Highlights
- **27 items and trinkets**: wearable gear, actives, familiars, and four **Q4 legends** (Championship Ring, Hall of Fame Jacket, Golden Arm, Hail Mary)
- **Six characters**: The Quarterback, The Kicker and The Coach, plus tainted versions that play completely differently
- **Three bosses, a miniboss and 10 football enemies** in Isaac's own grotesque style, every player in full kit (pads under the jersey, pants, socks, cleats), with **Team Captain** champions
- **Worn costumes** fitted per character (roomy helmets for big hair, horn holes for horns), in **11 team colours**, pink included
- **All-Pro** transformation, a **Halftime show** event, **completion notes**, and dozens of **base-game synergies**
- **Football in the basement**: some rooms are dressed with faded field paint, sideline junk, goalposts and benches
- **Juiced**: steroids make tackles brutal (enemies pinball into each other), but the referees run drug tests
- **Boss themes** for the Linebacker, The Mascot and The Franchise: marching drumlines, brass, organ and a dark choir
- **Stadium sounds**, a settings menu (press **C**), External Item Descriptions support, and save & continue

### Set bonus: Suited Up
Hold **3 or more** of the nine wearable gear items (Helmet, Pads, Jersey, Cleats, Eye Black, Gloves, Mouthguard, Padded Pants, QB Wristband) to get **Suited Up**: +1 Damage, the Helmet's tackle winds up twice as fast, and you pulse gold.

### Transformation: All-Pro
Hold **any 3 Gridiron items** (passives, actives, familiars, Q4s: anything from this mod) to become **All-Pro**, for the rest of the run like a vanilla transformation: **+0.5 Damage**, **every 4th shot is a homing golden spiral**, and your eye black turns to **gold war paint** (it shows through the facemask).

### Field decor
About one normal room in eight looks like somebody played football in it, and no two look alike:
- **Game field:** goalposts at both ends, end zones, yard lines and painted numbers.
- **Locker room:** a row of dented lockers along the wall (some hanging open), a whiteboard of plays, a bench, the shower drain and puddles, bloody towels and dropped jerseys.
- **Practice field:** a line of cones, a taped agility ladder, a tire drill, a sled and ball bags.
- **Turf field:** torn, filthy patches of old artificial turf laid over the floor.
- Or just a **sideline** down one wall, **end zone stripes** in a corner, **painted yard numbers**, a scrubbed **team logo**, or sideline junk (cups, stains, a tipped cooler, a busted pylon, a dented helmet, a bloody mouthguard).

The football bosses' rooms get **stadium banners**. Props never collide with anything or block a door, and each room looks the same every time you come back. Set **field decor** to off, light or full in *visuals*.

### Pro Shop
A quarter of shops (adjustable in the menu) are **team stores**: banners and lockers on the walls, and the first time you walk in, one item for sale is a **Gridiron item you don't have yet**, at the shop's price.

### Halftime show
Clearing a normal room has a **6% chance** (once per floor, adjustable in the menu) to start a **halftime show**: the whistle, the stadium horn, fireworks and the crowd. You get to **pick one**: a **Gridiron item you don't have yet** or a **soul heart**. Take one and the other disappears.

## Installing
1. Download `gridiron-vX.Y.Z.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/bulmaspanties/tboifbm/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it into your Isaac `mods` folder, so you end up with `mods/gridiron/metadata.xml`. On Steam/Windows that's
   `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\The Binding of Isaac Rebirth\mods`.
3. Launch the game and turn on **Gridiron** in the Mods menu.
4. To try everything at once, open the debug console (`~`) and type `gridiron give` and walk over the pedestals.

### Publishing to the Steam Workshop
The release zip is already laid out the way the Workshop uploader wants: one `gridiron/` folder with
`metadata.xml` (name, BBCode description, tags, version, visibility), `thumb.png` (the 512×512 preview) and only
the files the game loads. No build tools or tests ship in it.

1. Unzip the release into the game's `mods` folder, so you have `mods/gridiron/metadata.xml`.
   Launch the game once with the mod enabled to check it loads.
2. Run **ModUploader.exe** from the game's `tools/ModUploader` folder
   (`steamapps/common/The Binding of Isaac Rebirth/tools/ModUploader`), pick **gridiron** and upload.
   It fills in the name, description, tags and preview from the folder. Add a change note (copy the newest CHANGELOG entry).
3. `metadata.xml` ships with `<visibility>Private</visibility>`, so the first upload is only visible to you.
   Subscribe to it on another profile or check the page, then switch it to **Public** on the Workshop page (or in the file) when you're ready.
4. After the first upload the uploader writes the Workshop `<id>` into `mods/gridiron/metadata.xml`.
   **Copy that `<id>` line into the repo's `metadata.xml`**: every later upload then updates the same Workshop page instead of making a new one.
5. If the uploader rejects a tag, delete that `<tag>` line and upload again.
6. Optional: on the Workshop page, **Add/edit images & videos** and upload `docs/preview.gif` plus the
   `docs/*.png` sheets as extra screenshots. The GIF animates on the Workshop page.

To update: bump `<version>`, build the zip, unzip it over `mods/gridiron` (keep the `<id>`), and run the uploader again.

### Making a release
Bump `<version>` in `metadata.xml` and push to `main`, then run the **Release** workflow from the Actions tab (or push a matching `v*` tag).
The Release workflow runs the smoke test, builds the zip with `tools/package.py` and publishes it.

If **External Item Descriptions** is installed, every item shows its description (numbers follow your settings).

## Settings menu
Gridiron adds a page to the **Dead Sea Scrolls** menu (press **C** in a run, or the key you've set):
- **gameplay:** Gridiron items on/off and Q4 legends on/off (for new runs), tackle windup, facemask block %, pad deflect %, glove catch %, pants shrug-off %, yards per first down, kick damage, drug tests, Franchise boss chance, Mascot boss chance, Pro Shop chance, halftime show chance, Linebacker boss chance, football-enemy chance, reset to defaults
- **visuals:** costumes on/off, Suited Up glow, HUD text (down & distance, current play, bucket fill), popup text (callouts like TACKLE!, IT'S GOOD!, FUMBLE!), crowd sounds (cheers, boos, the stadium horn and the scoring fanfare), boss music, field decor (off / light / full)
- **uniform:** by default (**auto**) every character suits up in **their own colours**: Magdalene in pink and white, Judas in black and red, Cain in green and gold, Keeper in gold and money green, Blue Baby in blues, the tainted characters in darker, meaner versions, and so on. Or pick your team colours yourself: **helmet** shell, **jersey** (the trim and sleeve stripes match) and **pants**, eleven colours each (red, blue, navy, green, orange, purple, gold, black, silver, white, pink); **team defaults** puts everything back on auto. Helmet and Team Jersey **pedestals** show your colours too (except where the item could be hidden: Curse of the Blind and the alt-floor "?" treasure items)
- **completion notes:** the marks each Gridiron character has earned
- **costume fit:** hide the helmet, eye black, jersey/pads or pants/cleats for the character you're playing right now

Settings are saved and kept between runs. Your run state (caught balls, bucket, jersey, current play…) also survives **save & continue**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Costumes</b></summary>

## Costumes
Your character wears what you pick up: the helmet (with facemask) and eye black on the head, jersey and/or shoulder pads on the torso, and pants and/or cleats on the legs.
All costumes are managed in Lua, so they can be turned off, hidden per character, and are re-applied after anything that resets costumes.
They're drawn on Isaac's body shape, with fixes per character, sized from each character's sprite:
- **Big hair** (Magdalene, Eve, Samson, Bethany, Lilith, Lazarus, Eden and their tainted versions, plus Tainted Judas and Tainted Cain) gets a **roomy helmet**, 2px bigger all round. The biggest curls and pigtails still peek out the sides, like hair under a real helmet.
- **Horns** (Azazel, Lilith, Apollyon and their tainted versions) come up **through reinforced holes** in the shell instead of poking out from under it.
- **No legs** (The Lost, The Soul, Azazel and their tainted versions): no pants or cleats.

If a piece still looks wrong on a character, hide it from **costume fit** in the menu (or `gridiron hide <slot>`).

</details>

<summary><b>For modders: the Gridiron API</b></summary>

## For modders: the Gridiron API
Gridiron publishes a global `Gridiron` table (check `if Gridiron then ... end`):

```lua
Gridiron.Items / .Trinkets / .Cards / .Pills / .MonsterType / .MonsterVariants / .PlayerTypes
Gridiron.IsSuitedUp(player)   Gridiron.IsAllPro(player)   Gridiron.IsJuiced(player)
Gridiron.GearCount(player)    Gridiron.UniformColor(player, "helmet" | "jersey" | "pants")

Gridiron.AddSynergyNote(itemId, "Your item: what it does with this one")   -- shows in EID
Gridiron.AddFootballSwap(EntityType.ENTITY_X, Gridiron.MonsterVariants.LINEMAN)
Gridiron.SetCharacterColors(yourPlayerType, { "purple", "gold", "white" })  -- auto uniform
Gridiron.AddCallback("TACKLE", function(player, enemy, damage) end)
-- also "FIELD_GOAL" (player, position), "TOUCHDOWN" (player), "SUITED_UP" (player), "ALL_PRO" (player)
```

## Testing
`tests/smoke_test.lua` loads `main.lua` against a mock of the game's Lua API and runs every item through its callbacks.
It also checks that item names, costume paths and sprite/animation paths match the XML and the files on disk.
Run it from the repo root with Lua 5.3 or newer:
```
lua tests/smoke_test.lua
```

It runs over 2000 checks and catches typos, nil errors and broken wiring. Balance and game feel still need a real playtest.
